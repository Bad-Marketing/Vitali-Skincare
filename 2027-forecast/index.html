For the record:

Three launches are modeled on competitor floors, not Vitali history.

Bakuchiol Oil, Cleanser and Hydration Oil sold too little in 2026 to model, so they use the low end of DataDive competitors (14–37 units a month). That floor already matches or beats Vitali's best seller today, about 18 units a month. Treat those three as achievable only with launch ad support and fresh reviews. The cleanser benchmark comes from a March 2025 competitor set.

How the forecast is built
  • Current catalog baseline. 2026 monthly sales minus the five launch SKUs, with Nov–Dec 2026 estimated as Nov–Dec 2025 × the Jan–Oct year-over-year ratio (0.94). Base repeats that in 2027. Conservative assumes the 2026 decline continues (−6%). Upside assumes +15% from PPC and listing work.
  • Seasonality. Average of the 2025 and 2026 monthly shape. December and October run highest, June and July lowest. Launch products follow the same shape.
  • Products with history (Eyelash Serum, Face Cream) use their own in-stock run rate. Products without usable 2026 history use the low end of competitor monthly unit sales from DataDive, at Vitali's current price.
  • Advertising. Ad share, ROAS and TACOS come from MerchantSpring (snapshot Nov 12, 2026) for complete 2026 months with ad spend. Each 2027 month's forecast is split into organic and ad-attributed sales at that share, and ad spend is the ad-attributed part divided by 2026 ROAS. Picking a ROAS keeps that spend and organic sales fixed and recomputes ad-attributed sales as spend × ROAS. It doesn't model organic lift from ads or a different budget.
  • Not included: price changes, Prime Day and BFCM deal lifts beyond normal seasonality, stockouts, and cannibalization between Vitali SKUs. December 2025 sales of the launch SKUs were assumed equal to November 2025.
